Who I am

I’m a Product Designer with a background in Communication Design — I bring systems thinking to every interface I build. Over the past 4 years, I’ve worked across brand identity, content design, and digital products for educational platforms and media brands, turning complex challenges into clear, scalable visual systems.

My approach combines strong visual craft with user-centered problem solving: prototyping and testing interfaces in Figma, running user interviews, and applying frameworks like JTBD and user flows to shape better product decisions.

I care about the details — from the way a design system scales across a team, to the way a single onboarding flow feels to a first-time user. Whether I’m building from scratch or refining something that already exists, I look for the simplest, clearest path to a solution that works for both users and the business.

Currently open to Product Design and UI/UX roles — remote or relocation.
